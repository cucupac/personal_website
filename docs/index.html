Interests

I study intelligent decision-making under uncertainty: how agents form abstractions, synthesize programs, and choose actions to achieve goals.

My research interests include program synthesis, library learning, reinforcement learning, human cognition, applied mathematics and statistics, and optimal routing and execution in AMM markets.
